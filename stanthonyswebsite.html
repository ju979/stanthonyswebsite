About Us

Our Story, Faith & Mission

History

Founded in 1992 by the Sisters of St Anthony in Narok Town, our school started with 80 girls in two classrooms. Today we are one of the leading girls' boarding schools in Narok County, with modern dormitories, science labs, and a thriving alumni network.


Our Motto: Semper Altius

“Always Higher” - we challenge every girl to aim higher academically, spiritually and personally.

🎯 Mission

To provide holistic, quality education that empowers girls to excel academically and live with integrity.

👁️ Vision

To be a centre of excellence producing confident, God-fearing women leaders.

💎 Core Values

Discipline • Faith • Excellence • Service • Respect • Integrity

🏫 Sponsorship

Catholic Diocese of Ngong - St Anthony's Parish, Narok.
